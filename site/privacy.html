3. Looking up the current version

So that the version number and download links are always right, your browser asks GitHub's public release list (api.github.com) when the page opens. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details; no cookies are set and no other data is sent. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in correct, current download information (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for the transfer to the USA, section 2 applies. If the request is blocked, e.g. by an ad blocker, the page uses version information stored when it was published.

4. Stored language choice

When you switch the language, the page stores your choice (borgstudio-lang = en or de) in your browser's local storage so it applies on your next visit. It never leaves your computer. Storing it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); you can delete it at any time in your browser's settings.

5. Links to GitHub

Downloads, the guide (wiki), the source code and problem reports are on GitHub. When you follow these links, GitHub's privacy statement applies there.

6. Contact

If you email me, I process your address and message to answer (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or (b) for questions about a contract) and delete them when they are no longer needed for that and no retention obligation applies. Posts in the GitHub issues are public; GitHub's privacy statement applies to them.

7. The BorgStudio app

BorgStudio processes your data only on your computer and sends backups only to the storage you set up yourself. There is no account, no telemetry and no crash reporting. To look for updates – can be switched off under Tools → Settings – the app asks GitHub's public release list; GitHub receives your IP address. I receive no data from the app.
